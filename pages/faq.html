Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for courses?

To register for courses, log in to your StudentHub dashboard and navigate to the "Courses" section. Click the "Browse Available Courses" button to view the course catalog for the current semester. Select the courses you want to enroll in and click "Register." Your academic advisor must approve the selection if you are enrolling in more than six courses. Registration is open from the first week of each semester until the add/drop deadline, which is typically two weeks after classes begin.

How can I check my attendance?

Your attendance records are available under the "Attendance" section on your dashboard. You can view attendance for each individual course, see the percentage of classes attended, and identify any dates you were marked absent. Attendance is updated by your professors within 24 hours of each class session. If you believe there is an error in your attendance record, you can raise a dispute through the same page by clicking the "Report Discrepancy" button.

When are results published?

Mid-semester examination results are typically published within two weeks of the last exam date, and end-semester results are released within four weeks. You will receive an email notification when your results are available. You can view your detailed results — including subject-wise marks, grades, and CGPA — on the "Results" page of your dashboard. If you wish to apply for re-evaluation, you must do so within seven days of the result publication date.

How do I reset my password?

Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page and enter the email address associated with your StudentHub account. You will receive a password reset link via email within a few minutes. Click the link and follow the instructions to set a new password. For security, the reset link expires after 30 minutes. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact the IT helpdesk at support@studenthub.edu.in for further assistance.

Can I change my enrolled courses?

Yes, you can add or drop courses during the add/drop period, which is the first two weeks of each semester. Go to the "Courses" section on your dashboard, select the course you wish to drop, and click "Drop Course." To add a new course, browse the catalog and register for an available section. After the add/drop deadline, changes require written approval from your department head and may result in a "W" (Withdrawal) grade on your transcript.

How do I contact my professors?

You can find your professors' contact details on each course page under the "Instructor" section, which lists their email address and office hours. You can also send them a message directly through the StudentHub messaging system by clicking "Message Instructor" on the course page. For urgent academic matters, we recommend visiting during their posted office hours or sending an email with your student ID and course code in the subject line.

What is the minimum attendance requirement?

The university requires a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to be eligible to sit for the end-semester examinations. Students falling below 75% will receive a warning notification when attendance drops below 80%. If your attendance falls below the required threshold due to medical or other valid reasons, you may apply for an attendance waiver through the "Attendance" section by submitting supporting documents. The final decision on waivers rests with the Dean of Student Affairs.

How do I submit assignments online?

Navigate to the "Assignments" section on your dashboard, where you will see a list of all pending and past assignments. Click on the assignment you want to submit, then upload your file using the "Upload Submission" button. Accepted file formats include PDF, DOCX, and ZIP (for code projects), with a maximum file size of 25 MB. Make sure to submit before the deadline — late submissions may incur a penalty as specified by your instructor. You will receive a confirmation email once your submission is successfully recorded.

Still have questions?

If you could not find the answer you were looking for, feel free to reach out to us directly. Our support team is happy to help.
